Wavvy is an intelligent Chrome extension that spots captive portals on campus, hotel, and airport Wi-Fi, auto-fills your credentials, and gets you online before you’ve opened a tab.

See how it works Set it up

From joining Wi-Fi to online, with no clicks.

It asks one question

You join the network. Wavvy checks a tiny probe address that should answer “204”, which means you’re really online.

The portal answers instead

Instead of 204, the network intercepts and sends a redirect to its login page. That’s how a portal gives itself away.

It logs in, out of sight

In a background tab, your saved details fill the form, terms get ticked, and Connect gets pressed automatically.

It checks again, then closes up

When the answer is 204, the tab closes silently and a quiet notification tells you you’re connected.

Wavvy monitors connectivity quietly and checks again when you wake your laptop. It never guesses: after three failed logins it pauses for five minutes, so a wrong password can’t lock you out. Your credentials stay 100% in your local browser sandbox.

Set it up in three steps.

  1. Open ExtensionsGo to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Load the folderChoose Load unpacked and select the Wavvy extension folder.
  3. Add your loginOpen Wavvy’s Settings, enter your username and password, and save.
